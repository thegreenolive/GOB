# 02.2 Types of Models

Models characterize a situation and the relationships within it. There is no standard way to categorize models, but in general terms, they fall into four categories:

* **Verbal Models**: These are narratives or written descriptions of a problem. For example, the capitalist model is an economic model based on private ownership of the means of production, competitive markets, and profit maximization.
* **Schematic Models:** These include pictures, charts, and graphics to visualize data or processes. A chart explaining the pendulum mechanism of a clock (Figure 02.1) is a good example of a schematic model. Students are challenged to identify the kinds of simplifications the model in the Figure.
