# 03.1 Introduction

In the real world, when crafting a program to solve a particular problem, programmers use their own methodology to tackle the issue. Most methodologies are personal; coders built their way into problem-solving after years of experience confronting challenges day after day. The main idea of this section is to teach students a simple methodology that can serve as a foundation for developing their own methodology in the years to come (even if that methodology involves AI agents).

The method focuses on the correct formulation of the problem and takes into account the methodology and the identification of its inputs and outputs. By focusing on a framework rather than chance, learning to code as a tool moves away from "intuitive" fixes and toward a strong, flexible foundation in problem-solving concepts.
