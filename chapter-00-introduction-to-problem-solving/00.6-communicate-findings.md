# 00.6 Communicate findings

This is the “so what?” phase. In most organizations, implementing a solution is not the responsibility of a single person. A board, team, or crew must agree on the solution and its implementation. The solution will be effective only if it is shared with stakeholders. For Lucy, this might mean updating her professors on her new routine; for Ray, it might involve sharing the wall construction timeline with his neighbor to mitigate future property disputes.

The solution must be synthesized and communicated in a way that stakeholders can understand and participate in the process. Technical details should be interpreted and presented appropriately depending on the audience. For example, Ray will describe his project in a way that follows the city's procedures and HOA formalism, and is friendly enough for neighbors.
