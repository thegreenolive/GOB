# 00.7 The big picture

Problem-solving is a skill that requires not only experience but also a diverse range of experience. The number and diversity of problems a person solves (or tries to solve), the better prepared she will be to solve new and complex ones. The notion of iteration also applies to the personal approach to apply the methodology shown in this section, a sort of meta-iteration to improve the problem-solving approach. Additionally, the circumstances of the problems affect, for example, whether iteration may be possible for those involving irreversible actions.

Figure 6 shows the iterative model to solve problems explained in this section. The middle node, “Redo,” indicates that at any given moment, your working solution can return to any previous step. The solution methodology is iterative by character.
