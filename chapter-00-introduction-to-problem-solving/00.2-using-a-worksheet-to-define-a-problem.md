# 00.2 Using a Worksheet to define a problem

Ill-defined problems are harder to solve. Therefore, the first step towards a solution is to define the problem. For example, check Problem 1. Before continuing, try to define the problem, assuming you want to find a solution.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Lucy just began college, and most of her classes are at 8:00 AM. The University is an urban one, and she lives alone in a studio close to campus but still commutes by public transportation. By the third week, she finds herself late for her early meetings too many times. This could impact her performance in those courses and even her grades.
{% endhint %}

<p align="center"><strong>Problem 1</strong></p>

Replace vague problem statements by following a definition process. Taking notes on the problem is a useful way to define it. When defining a problem, you could use the following checklist:

```
1. What is the thing/process you want to change or create?
2. What are the people who have a say on the state of affairs (stakeholders)?
3. What are the external actors that may influence stakeholders?
4. How do you know when you have solved the problem?
5. What is the deadline for the solution to be achieved?
```

Using the checklist on Problem 1, the result would be as follows.

1. What is the thing/process you want to change or create?

* Lucy wants to be on time for his morning classes.

2. What are the people who have a say on the state of affairs (stakeholders)?

* Lucy is the primary stakeholder whose academic standing may be at risk.
* Professors/Instructors dictate attendance policies and grading rubrics.
* Classmates are affected by Lucy's late arrival to lectures or group discussions.
* University Administrators set the fixed start times for the courses.

3. What are the external factors that may influence stakeholders?

* Public transit authorities oversee scheduling, delays, or service strikes.
* Local Traffic and Weather impact transit reliability

4. How do you know when you have solved the problem?

* Arriving at the classroom at least 5 minutes prior to the start of the lectures to allow for settling in, regardless of minor transit fluctuations.

5. What is the deadline for the solution to be achieved?

* Ideally, the solution must be implemented before the fourth week.
